# Personalisation: what a person's choices change

Spiritpedia can be tailored in three ways, all optional:

- **More of**: subjects someone wants to see first.
- **Never show**: subjects someone would rather not see at all.
- **Learning from what you explore** (§6): with their permission, the
  subjects someone opens, watches, saves and searches for most rank just
  below More of.

People choose them once at onboarding, straight after the first-launch intro,
and can change them any time in **Account → Tailor Spiritpedia** (also in the
menu). Both use the same picker: the five pillars, each opening into its
subjects, with "All of [pillar]" first. The pills and chips carry no icons or
symbols, so they read exactly like the Discover pills: violet when chosen for
More of, rose for Never show.

Tailor Spiritpedia is the star's space. The star arrives with the page and
floats at the top, typing: "Entirely optional, and never shared. Your choices
only shape what you see in Spiritpedia. Change them anytime below." The
choices scroll underneath it; nothing is blocked. Once it has finished it can
be swiped away, or it glides down to rest at the foot of the page. "Don't show
me again" stops it for good. With Reduce Motion the words appear without
typing. Saving closes any open Explore More shelves.

If someone chooses nothing, or skips, and learning is off, **the app behaves
exactly as it does for everyone else.** Nothing on this page applies to them.

A subject can be in **More of** or **Never show**, never both. An item can
carry subjects from both lists; then **More of wins** and it is shown (see §2).

---

## 1. Ranking, in four levels

Wherever something is ranked for a person, it goes in this order:

1. **Their subjects (More of)**: anything carrying a subject they chose.
2. **What they explore (learned)**: anything carrying one of the subjects
   Spiritpedia has learned they explore most (§6). Only with learning on.
3. **The rest of the More of pillars**: anything carrying another subject from
   the same pillar as one they chose.
4. **Everything else**, in its usual order.

Within each level the usual order is kept (newest first, best fit for a
pillar shelf, and so on), except: in level 1, the item carrying more of their
chosen subjects comes first; in level 2, the item carrying the most-explored
subjects comes first. Someone with learning on and no More of gets levels 2
and 4 only.

**Ranking never hides anything.** It only changes the order; every item is
still there.

### Example

Someone chose only **Reiki** (in Emotional Healing).

1. Reiki first.
2. Then, if learning is on and they have mostly been exploring Astrology and
   Yoga: those.
3. Then the rest of Emotional Healing: Energy Medicine, Quantum Touch,
   Self-Healing, Shadow Work, EFT Tapping, Breathwork and Conscious
   Relationships.
4. Then everything else.

### Where the ranking applies

| Place | What changes |
|---|---|
| **Discover: For You** | A new first shelf, one content type per visit, in rotation: Free Resources → Courses & Programmes → Downloads & Audio → Retreats & Live Events → Memberships. Labelled "For You · Free Resources" and so on. Levels 1 to 3, up to 20; level 4 is not in it. With learning on and no More of, it shows level 2. Retreats are upcoming only (starting today or later), soonest first within each level. A type with nothing in levels 1–2 is skipped. Teachers are not in it: they have the shelves below. |
| **Discover: every teacher shelf** (the billboard, Worldwide, Rising Voices, Practitioners Near You, Timeless Teachers, Explore These Channels) | Each is re-ordered by the four levels. The same teachers are on each shelf. |
| **Discover: the pill row** | The pillars holding their subjects come first, most chosen first; then those holding learned subjects, most explored first. The pills themselves are unchanged. |
| **Videos and Books: the pillar shelves** | The shelves holding their subjects (then their learned subjects) move to the top, straight after "New to Spiritpedia". Each pillar shelf, and the mixed shelf at the end, is re-ordered by the four levels. |
| **Spiritpedia Suggests** (search) | A small bonus: 20 per chosen subject (up to 60) for level 1, 15 for level 2, 10 for level 3. It settles a close call between two relevant items, but never beats a title match and never suggests something irrelevant. |

### Never re-ordered by More of or learning

- **"New to Spiritpedia"** on Videos and Books stays newest first.
- **Publishing Houses** keeps its order.
- **Explore More** sections (Free Resources, Courses & Programmes, Retreats &
  Live Events, Downloads & Audio, Memberships) keep their order. Retreats stay
  soonest first.
- **A chosen subject filter.** When someone picks a subject from the pills,
  they see that subject, in its usual order.
- **Search results** (names and titles) keep their order. Only Suggests is
  ranked.

## 2. Never show: what is hidden

### The rules

- **An item is hidden if any one of its subjects is hidden.** Items are
  videos, books, courses, retreats, downloads, memberships and free
  resources. For example, a video tagged *Death & the Afterlife* and
  *Shamanism* is hidden for someone who hid *Shamanism*.
- **More of beats Never show.** An item carrying any More of subject is shown,
  even if it also carries a hidden one. Someone who chose *Reiki* and hid
  *Shamanism* still sees a video tagged both.
- **A teacher, channel or publisher stays visible unless every subject they
  cover is hidden.** Their own items still follow the item rule, so a teacher's
  page can show the teacher but not some of their videos.
- **A teacher or channel whose content is all hidden is hidden too**, so no
  one sees an empty profile. Their content is everything their page would
  show: videos, books, and live courses, retreats, downloads, memberships and
  free resources, each judged by the item rule (More of included). A teacher
  with no content at all follows the subject rule only. Publishers follow the
  subject rule only.
- **Something with no subjects is never hidden.**

### When hiding empties a view

A single shelf that hiding empties simply disappears. When a **whole view** is
left with nothing (a tab, a pill's view, a subject page, or an opened Explore
More shelf), it shows the star with "You've hidden most subjects." and a link,
"Adjust in Tailor Spiritpedia". Never a blank screen.

### Where hiding applies

| Place | What is hidden |
|---|---|
| **Discover** | Teachers, channels and publishers whose every subject is hidden, and teachers and channels whose content is all hidden, on every shelf (the billboard included). In For You and Explore More, every item with a hidden subject and no More of subject. |
| **Videos and Books** | Every video or book with a hidden subject, on every shelf ("New to Spiritpedia" included). A pillar shelf left empty disappears. |
| **The pill row** | A hidden subject is left out of its pillar's list. A pillar whose every subject is hidden is left out of the row. |
| **A teacher's or publisher's page** | Their videos, books, courses and free resources with a hidden subject. |
| **A subject page** | Its items with a hidden subject. If the subject itself is hidden, the page says "You've hidden this subject. Show anyway?" and shows nothing until they tap it. |
| **Spiritpedia Suggests** | Never suggests an item with a hidden subject (unless it also carries a More of subject, as everywhere). |
| **Search: names and titles** | Hidden items are held back. If the search found any, a quiet note says "You've hidden this subject. Show anyway?", and one tap shows them for that search. |
| **Search: feelings** ("I feel lost") | Items with a hidden subject are left out, with no note: the person didn't ask for them by name. |

### Never hidden

- **The crisis intercept.** It runs first, on every keystroke, exactly as
  before. Hiding plays no part in it.
- **My Library.** Something saved stays saved and visible, even if one of its
  subjects is hidden later.
- **A page opened directly**, from a link, a notification, a share, My Library,
  or "Show anyway" in search. The page itself opens normally.
- **Reviews**, and anything on the website. The website does not use these
  choices yet.

## 4. Your area: Practitioners Near You

An area is a **town and its country** (public.places, migration 0019: GeoNames
towns of 5,000+ people). Never a position: "Use my location" takes one
approximate position, rounds it to about 1 km, asks `nearest_place` for the
town, and keeps only the town. Practitioners get their town from their city
and country automatically (`healers.place_id`, by trigger).

| No area | Area set |
|---|---|
| Everyone, as before, with **"Set your area"** under the title | 1. In person **within 50 km**, nearest first. 2. Then everyone who offers sessions **online** (availability says "Online"), from anywhere. Local-only practitioners elsewhere are left out. "Near London, United Kingdom · Change"; if nobody is near: "No practitioners near London yet — these offer online sessions." |

Within each group the usual order is kept (More of first), and Never show
still applies. The same rules run on the website (`web/utils/area.js`) and in
the app (`shelf_rules.dart` → `nearYou`); change both together.

Where it is set: the shelf's "Set your area" / "Change", **Tailor Spiritpedia →
Your area**, and the star's question (around the 5th visit; see
`lib/features/star/conductor.dart`). Kept like the choices below.

## 5. Where the choices are kept

- **On the phone, straight away**, so they work signed out.
- **In the account when signed in** (`user_interests`, migrations 0017 and
  0018), so they follow the person to a new phone. Choices made before signing
  in are attached at sign-in. If both the phone and the account have choices,
  **the account wins**.
- **Only the person can read or change them.** Nobody else can, and there is
  no admin access. They are never shared or used for advertising.
- **Signing out** clears them from the phone; they stay in the account.
  **Deleting the account** deletes them.
- **Your area** is kept the same way (user_preferences, migration 0019), on
  the phone and in the website's browser storage.

- **What has been learned** lives only in the account (subject_affinity,
  migration 0021), never on the phone, and only while learning is on (§6).

## 6. Learning from what you explore

**Off unless someone turns it on**, and only for a signed-in member: "Let
Spiritpedia learn from what I explore". Underneath, always: "Used only to
tailor what you see. Never shown to our team, never shared, never used for
advertising. Turn off anytime."

**Where it's asked and kept.** The star asks once, from the 3rd visit, signed
in only, while it's off: "Shall I learn from what you explore, to put more of
what you love first?" with "Yes, learn" or "Not now" (the conductor's rules:
one appearance a session; put away, once more at least 15 visits and 3 weeks
later, then never). It comes before the area (5th visit) and name (7th)
questions. The switch lives in **Tailor Spiritpedia**, under Never show, and on
the website's account page. It saves the moment it's changed.

**What counts**, and how much, per subject the item carries:

| Signal | Weight |
|---|---|
| Opening an item's page (once per item per session) | 1 |
| Starting a video (a touch on the player, or "Watch on YouTube") | 2 |
| Saving | 4 |
| Unsaving | −4 (never below 0) |
| Sharing | 3 |
| A new review | 3 |
| A subject pill (Discover) or filter (Videos, Books) | 1 |
| A search that settled: each subject it led to (top 3) | 1 |

A search never sends its words for learning, only the subjects it led to.
**A search the crisis intercept caught is never sent at all**: the search only
reports a search that passed both safety gates, a moment after its results
settle, and the website checks again. Never show subjects are never scored.

**Fading.** Each subject's score halves every 30 days. A subject counts as
learned from a score of 3 (a save, say, or three opens); the top 5 are level 2
of the ranking. More of subjects are left out (they rank first already), and
so are Never show ones: **learning never brings back what someone hid, and
never hides anything.**

**Off deletes.** Turning it off deletes every event and score at once (a
database trigger), and ranking goes back to their choices alone. Signed out,
nothing personal is recorded.

**Kept for.** Each event 30 days; the scores until learning is turned off or
the account is deleted.

**Totals for everyone.** The same signals, from everyone (signed in or out,
learning on or off), are also added to anonymous daily counts — no account,
device or IP — behind the admin's Activity page: most opened, most saved,
rising subjects, searches that found nothing, sign-ups. The admin sees totals,
never one person's browsing. Popularity only: nothing commercial orders
anything.

**Batching.** Events queue on the phone and go to the website's
`/api/activity` every 30 seconds, at 20 events, or when the app goes to the
background; they wait on the phone if they can't go. The learned subjects are
read once a session.
