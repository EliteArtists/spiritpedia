Bio:

Awaken Village Press is proof that a publishing house can be built like a garden. Founded by author and “book doula” Amanda Johnson, this small independent press was born from her own experience of writing her memoir, Becoming Free — and the discovery at the heart of it: that stories are not birthed alone. They need community, care, and courage. From that knowing grew the Village: a companion publisher whose mission is nothing smaller than awakening the planet, one book at a time, and whose method is the opposite of the industry’s — books, they say, are not manufactured here; they are midwifed.

Everything about the Village is deliberately different. Where traditional publishing is extractive — value flowing up, authors left behind — Awaken Village runs on what it calls a regenerative model, a living ecosystem in which authors, readers, and patrons all belong and value circulates back to sustain new voices. The team even carries nature’s names for their roles: Amanda as the Sun of the Village, tending the vision; editors and designers as Honeybees of Language and Design; and, in the loveliest detail of all, Amanda’s own mother, Mer, as the Earth — grounding every manuscript in thirty-five years of meticulous care. Authors are stewarded, never owned, walked from first word to publication and beyond through the Authors’ Village community, the Author Incubator, and hands-on guidance at every step.

The books that emerge carry exactly the medicine such a process would promise: raw, brave, transformational stories of healing and becoming — memoirs of trauma and liberation, guides to spiritual and financial sovereignty, tales told “warts and all” so that readers feel seen. For Spiritpedia, Awaken Village Press holds a special place: it is the home of emerging voices — the local heroes of the spiritual world — and living evidence that the future of sacred storytelling isn’t only built in Carlsbad and Colorado, but in villages: small, devoted, and growing.
