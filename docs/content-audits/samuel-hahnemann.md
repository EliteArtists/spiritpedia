What actually holds his legacy. There are three bodies with a genuine claim, and they do very different things:

1. Institut für Geschichte der Medizin der Robert Bosch Stiftung (IGM), Stuttgart — https://www.igm-bosch.de/ — physically holds Hahnemann's estate: his case journals, his ~6,000 patient letters, and the papers of his second wife Mélanie d'Hervilly. This is the archive of record, and my recommended Booking URL.
2. Hahnemann-Edition — https://www.hahnemann-edition.de/ — the IGM's free, open-licence digital critical edition of his case journals. Not a shopfront: a real scholarly edition under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
3. The Hahnemann House Trust — https://www.hahnemannhouse.org/ — UK charity no. 252702, founded 1920, now trusteed by Homeopathy UK. A heritage and artefact collection rather than a manuscript archive, but English-language and far more public-facing.

Official YouTube presence: none for the archive. The IGM has no YouTube channel at all and deliberately self-hosts its lecture video and audio on its own server at https://www.igm-bosch.de/mediathek.html. The Hahnemann House Trust has no YouTube and no social media of any kind. What does exist are two small German memorial associations — the Internationales Hahnemannzentrum Torgau (his home 1805–1811) and the Meißner Hahnemannzentrum (his birth city) — whose channels I enumerated directly in a browser. Between them they hold 15 videos, of which 4 are actually about Hahnemann.

EDITORIAL FLAGS FOR ROSS
1. THIS RECORD DOES NOT FIT THE ASCENDED-MASTER MODEL, AND THAT IS THE MAIN FINDING. Every other master in this sprint died within living memory and left a foundation that sells their teaching. Hahnemann died in 1843. There is no estate, no successor organisation, no archive membership, no paid programme carrying his name. What exists instead is a research archive, two small German memorial associations, a UK heritage charity, and a vast public-domain literature. Three of four sections cannot honestly reach 20, and the shortfalls are not research failures — they are the shape of the subject. Decision needed: accept a short record, or change how pre-modern figures are handled in the schema.

1. Booking URL — pick between two. I recommend igm-bosch.de because it physically holds his estate and is the authoritative body. But it is a German-language academic institute with no visitor offering, which may read oddly as a "booking" link. hahnemannhouse.org is English, public-facing, artefact-rich and free — a better user experience, a weaker claim to being the estate. Your call.

1. VIDEOS REACH 4. The archive that holds his papers has no YouTube channel at all and deliberately self-hosts its video on its own server — so any "official Hahnemann channel" anyone offers you is not that body. I enumerated all three memorial-association channels directly in a browser: Torgau has 4 videos across two channels (all Hahnemann-related, all listed), Meissen has 11 (almost all about local festivals and a monastery, listed separately so you can see them but not recommended). Four further videos are embedded on the official Torgau site but owned by a private channel — institutional endorsement, private ownership — listed for your decision.

1. BOOKS REACH 10, AND STRICTLY IT IS 6 WORKS. Records 1, 5 and 6 are three translations of the same book, the Organon — the field genuinely treats them as distinct, which is why I listed them, but if your database counts works rather than editions the honest total is six. One is Latin-only with no English translation in existence. Three titles that sound like separate books — Aesculapius in the Balance, Friend of Health, On the Venereal Disease — have no purchasable English edition at all; the first exists only as a chapter in The Lesser Writings. There is no collected-works edition in English.

1. PAID OFFERINGS REACH 12, AND THE ARCHIVE IS FREE. Total verified monetised output across every legacy body: four books and a DVD under €70, a €60/year association membership, a £99 beginner course, two €69.90 scholarly volumes. Three museums exist but publish no admission price anywhere I could reach. If this section needs volume it can only come from the general homeopathy market, which I have listed separately and labelled rather than blending in.

1. World of Books is NULL on almost everything, and there is a trap here. Their author-collection page returns placeholder slugs that look like real product URLs and 404 when followed — the same failure I hit on the Grof record. Only one WoB URL survived verification, and the listings that could be confirmed are out of stock. Do not let anyone pattern-construct WoB slugs from ISBNs for these titles.

1. One genuinely excellent find worth highlighting. hahnemann-edition.de is not a publisher shopfront — it is a real, free, open-licence scholarly edition of Hahnemann's own case journal from his Paris years, published by the institute that holds the manuscript. You can read his actual consultation notes. That is the strongest single free asset in this record and what I featured.

1. What is gated. The largest body of transcribed case-journal text sits behind a sign-in at datenbanken.igm-bosch.de, though a guest sign-in with no email signup is offered — borderline rather than a hard paywall, and excluded per your rule. Archive access to the physical papers is by application form. Original manuscript page images could not be confirmed as freely viewable anywhere; treat free facsimile access as unproven. Note also that not every Internet Archive Hahnemann result is open — at least one Organon there is borrow-only — so items need checking individually.

1. Project Gutenberg is a dead end, which surprised me. Its Hahnemann author page carries essentially one German title. Neither the Organon, nor Chronic Diseases, nor Materia Medica Pura is there. Internet Archive and Wellcome are where the free full texts actually live.

1. Verification method. All igm-bosch.de, hahnemann-edition.de, hahnemannhouse.org, Wellcome, Internet Archive and KVC Verlag URLs were fetched live. The three memorial-association YouTube channels were enumerated directly in a browser session, which is how the honest video count was established. Amazon and Goodreads block automated fetching, so those URLs are verbatim search-result matches. Four Amazon .co.uk URLs were unavailable and .com was used instead; none were constructed.
