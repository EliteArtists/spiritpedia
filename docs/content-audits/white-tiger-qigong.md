HEALER PROFILE

Name: Tevia Feng
Slug: tevia-feng
Tier: luminary
Bio: Tevia Feng was born with chronic health problems and started qigong, martial arts and meditation as a boy, at first simply to cope. Later, a car accident left him with a serious spinal injury, and he made disciplined daily practice the centre of his recovery. That practice became his life's work. He trained with Dr. Yang Jwing-Ming in the early 2000s, then spent years with teachers across China and Southeast Asia. In Daoist lineages on Wuyi Mountain he studied with Abbot Luo Pin Cho of the Tian Ji Dao sect and Abbot Xu Zhong of Zhi Zhi An Temple. He learned Wudang forms under Master Yuan Xiu Gang and Baguazhang in the Sha Guo Zheng tradition in Yunnan. He also studied with Mantak Chia in Thailand and Edy Suwita in Sumatra, and worked in a medical qigong clinic in China under Dr. Ming Jie Wu.

His teaching draws on many transmissions: Medical and Golden Elixir Qigong, Five Element and Hua Tuo's Five Animal forms, Eight Trigram Qigong, the Microcosmic Orbit, Nei Dan internal alchemy and the monastic Brocades. He founded White Tiger Qigong in 2008 to make this material accessible without diluting it. He shaped the curriculum around three areas: Chinese medicine, fascia and biomechanics, and neuroscience. He says he worked with education, sports-science and performance specialists to set the school's certification standards. He is fluent in Mandarin, has self-published three books on qigong, and is published by YMAA. YMAA's author page describes him as having been named one of the top three qigong masters in Indonesia.

Today he leads the school's teacher trainings and retreats in Vietnam. He also takes a small number of private clients through his "Prescription System." It starts with a discovery session, then combines a Chinese-medicine constitutional reading (BaZi chart, face reading and a five-element assessment) with a personal movement prescription.

## NOTES

**Directory mechanics.** The directory has no individual instructor pages. Each "instructor page" is a bio panel that expands inside the directory card. No card links to a website or social account, so every external URL in Part 3 came from the instructor's own site or an organiser page they control.

**Figures that conflict across official pages.** The homepage says "teaching since 2008", but the About page says "40+ years of practice" and Who We Are says "20+ years of teaching." The About page says "taught in 20+ countries", but the homepage says "80+ countries." The About and Who We Are pages say "200+ certified instructors", but the directory lists 165. YMAA's older author page says "almost 60,000 members." The location is also unclear: the contact FAQ says Chiang Mai, Thailand in one place and Vietnam in another, and Tevia's card says Thailand. None of these change the tiers.

**Claims kept out of the bios.** The About page says Tevia was the first person authorised to design a qigong programme for Bhutan's central monastic body (it says details are "forthcoming"). It also names Harvard, Stanford, Cirque du Soleil and European Ballet specialists as collaborators, and describes use in cancer-focused and hospital settings. None of this is independently verified, and the Who We Are page names no institutions, so I described the collaborations only in general terms. The spinal-injury story is told as his own path, without claiming qigong healed it.

**Per-person flags**
- **Sarah Divine:** Her own site links only Facebook. The Instagram URL comes from her Humanitix organiser profile ("Hosted by Sarah Divine Qigong"), an account she controls. Drop it to NULL if you only accept links from a person's own website. Her email address is on a whitetigerqigong.com domain but is published on her own site, so I included it.
- **Ariel Tang:** Her site arieltang.com is confirmed through her Jivamukti teacher page. That page lists older handles (be_love_ariel); I used the current ones from her own site footer. Her site has repositioned her as an ascension mentor and trance channel, and no longer mentions yoga or qigong; the bio reflects both sides. White Tiger lists her location as Indonesia (Ubud), Jivamukti says Hong Kong, and her company is a UAE free-zone LLC, so I chose worldwide availability. Her follower counts could not be checked because the browser extension failed. She is local_hero unless you find 50K+ on one platform.
- **Isaac Osborne:** motionunlimited.net does not name him on its homepage. The link between him and the site comes from the Santa Barbara Independent and from his own White Tiger bio, which names Motion Unlimited. No socials were found.
- **Vince Sauter:** His site shows vsauter@optonline.net only as a PayPal address, not as a contact email, so I left it NULL. Add it if your rules count it. His location is his mailing address in Salt Point, NY; classes run at the Clinton Community Library and on Zoom.
