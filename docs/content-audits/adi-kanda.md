Bio:
Adi Kanda’s story begins on a cold January morning in 2000, when she opened her eyes to find pages of writing in her bedside journal — writing she had no memory of producing. That spontaneous awakening opened a channel that has never closed: in the twenty-five years since, Adi has received thousands of sacred, poetic transmissions, moving over time from sleep-state writing into conscious reception, and building alongside them a quarter-century practice of deep energy work — releasing, as she describes it, karmic patterns at a cellular level. Her signature Kore readings have reached clients worldwide, many of whom describe them as liberating beyond any modality they have known; her transmissions have been shared in workshops, performances, and salons, carrying what listeners describe as an activating frequency that opens the heart and often brings them to tears.
An author, intuitive, practitioner, and mother of three based in Toronto, Adi gathered the first curated harvest of her life’s receiving into Dear Human Child: Love Letters From the Divine (2025) — Book One of a planned trilogy for what she calls our days of the great Shift. Direct, nuanced, and profound, the writings weave metaphysical wisdom with literary grace — examining duality and division, sexual life-force, death and rebirth, and the courage to unravel our karmic wounds — and her publisher places the book squarely in the lineage of the Tao Te Ching, A Course in Miracles, and Conversations with God: words meant to be read aloud, contemplated, and embodied. More than a book, she offers it as energy medicine — whispers, as she frames it, from our Creator to our deepest heart.
