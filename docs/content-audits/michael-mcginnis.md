Bio:
Michael McGinnis — “The Jungle Writer” — has dedicated his life to the awakening of humanity, and he came to that mission by a road as wild as his fiction: a law-breaking high-school dropout who turned his life around in Central America, where he built a reputation as an entrepreneur, big-wave surfer, quarter-mile race-car driver, and the unofficial sheriff of the small town he helped develop. From the jungle he began transmitting the story he felt the world needed: The Essential Revolution (2020), winner of the Literary Titan Gold Book Award — a sweeping visionary novel that travels from ghetto housing projects to the seats of government power, following souls like Marcus Ogabi, a Black American cop in crisis, and the mysterious indigenous elder Ana, who is always one step ahead.
But this is no ordinary revolution: the battle in Michael’s fiction is the inner one — love against fear, awakening against control — and readers finish it feeling the story was secretly about them. Book Two, The Invitation, continues the saga, with The Summoning on its way, while Michael’s off-page life matches his pages: building conscious community and backing reforestation projects from his jungle home. He is Spiritpedia’s first voice of visionary fiction — proof that a novel can be a spiritual teaching wearing a thriller’s clothes.
