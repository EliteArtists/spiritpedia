Shaka is a psychic, Reiki Master, and E-RYT 500 yoga teacher from London, now rooted on the Caribbean coast of Costa Rica — a practitioner whose seventeen years as student, teacher, and healer have woven three traditions into one unhurried, deeply attentive way of working. People come to her for wisdom, guidance and oracle on the matters of their lives; for energetic and spiritual attunement and healing; or simply for relaxation, meditation and yoga — and she meets each of them compassionately, holding space to explore and ask questions rather than performing answers at them.
