Source of truth for the email and star changes agreed on 21 October 2026.

- `[first name]` — use "Hi [first name]," when a first name exists at send time, otherwise "Hello,". Never show an unfilled placeholder. Never require a name to send.
- `[PROFILE LINK]`, `[CLAIM LINK]` — real working URLs.
