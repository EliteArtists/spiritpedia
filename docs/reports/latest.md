**Bio (draft):** Insight Timer is a free meditation app and audio library used
by around 30 million people. It began as a simple meditation timer released on
Apple's App Store in 2009, and was taken over by Australian brothers
Christopher and Nicholas Plowman, who built it into a platform run by Insight
Network Inc. Its library holds more than 300,000 free guided meditations,
talks and music tracks from around 20,000 teachers in over 50 languages, from
mindfulness, yoga nidra and breathwork to Buddhist and non-dual teaching,
chanting and sound. Alongside the free library there are daily live sessions,
multi-day courses and in-person retreats. Member Plus (US$59.99 a year) adds
the courses, offline listening and higher-quality audio, and helps keep the
library free; listeners can also donate to teachers directly.

**Offerings:** Member Plus, $59.99 USD a year with a 7-day trial
(insighttimer.com/member-plus); Retreats, from $247 USD
(insighttimer.com/retreats).
**Free resources:** the free meditation timer, the free guided-meditation
library, free live sessions, the techniques directory. Its own marketing leans
on anxiety, stress and insomnia: none of that in our text, and its /sleep and
therapist pages are skipped.

## 3. The pipeline

profiles and the public A–Z directory 12,492. Most aren't English, or are
music, therapy or tiny accounts. No public page ranks teachers by followers,
so the pool is Insight Timer's own "Popular teachers" lists on 47 spiritual
topic pages: **581 teachers**.

## 4. Rules for the sprint

The Shift brief: no health claims, never anything "medical", at most 3
subjects, email required, tier from evidence, original bios. Every teacher
gets one free resource, "**<Name> on Insight Timer**", linking to their
profile (as Frans Stiene has). No track is ever copied.
